Privacy Policy — B2M2 Studio

Effective October 20, 2026

B2M2 Studio is a creation and listing tool by MUNIComb. It helps you design products and publish and manage listings on marketplaces you connect, such as eBay, Etsy, and Printify. This policy explains what the app can access, why, and the choices you keep.

What the app accesses

When you connect a marketplace account, you authorize B2M2 Studio through that marketplace's own sign-in and consent screen (OAuth). With your permission, the app can access only what it needs to work:

  • Your marketplace account basics (such as your seller username and account policies for fulfillment, payment, and returns)
  • Your inventory and listings, so the app can create, edit, and manage them for you
  • Your orders, so the app can show them to you and alert you to new sales
  • Product designs and images you create or choose inside the app

The app never sees your marketplace password. Sign-in happens on the marketplace's own pages, and access is granted with a revocable token.

Where your data lives

B2M2 Studio is local-first. Your designs, listing drafts, and connection tokens are stored on your own device. When the app talks to a marketplace, it does so directly between your device and that marketplace's official API, using the access you granted.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and the app carries no advertising trackers.
  • We do not publish, change, or delete anything in your marketplace accounts except as you direct in the app.
  • We do not access anything beyond the permissions shown on the marketplace consent screen.

Your control

You can disconnect a marketplace at any time, inside the app or from the marketplace's own connected-apps settings. Disconnecting revokes the app's access immediately. You may also request deletion of any data associated with your use of B2M2 Studio by contacting us through municomb.com, and we will honor the request.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

B2M2 Studio is a MUNIComb product. Questions about this policy can be sent through municomb.com.
